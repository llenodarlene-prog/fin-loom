## Interlinking (before and after)

- **Before:** 15 of 27 live posts had no inbound body link. Most posts linked only to their category page, which does not count under the SOP.
- **After:** every live post has at least 2 inbound body links. Every spoke links to its pillar and carries 2 to 3 post links. Every pillar links to all of its live spokes.
- **Category links:** body links to category pages were removed or repointed to the pillar.
- **Linking sentences:** each new sentence describes only what the linked post covers. Each was re-read against the linked post, and no new factual claims were added.
- **Dates:** `modified` was set to 2026-10-18 on all 27 posts because their body text changed.

## Technical and schema

- **llms.txt:** `/llms.txt` is now built from live posts, grouped by silo. Scheduled posts appear only on their publish date.
- **Hub pages:** each is a `CollectionPage` with `hasPart` listing its live posts, plus a `BreadcrumbList`.
- **Articles:** each article declares `isPartOf` its hub.
- **Organization:** the Organization node now carries `description`, `slogan` and the approved contact `email`.
- **Breadcrumbs:** non-article pages (about, contact, legal, hubs) now carry a `BreadcrumbList`.
- **Already in place:** FAQPage schema from each FAQ section, a connected `@graph` with `@id` references, canonical and og tags on every page, the sitemap with `lastmod`, and production robots.txt with the Sitemap line.

## New checks

- **`npm run check:silo`:** enforces SOP step 4 on live posts. It is part of `npm run verify` and the production release gate.
- **Repository test:** the old "exactly one internal link" test was replaced with the silo role, pillar and 2-to-3-link rules.

## Results

- **`npm run verify`:** passes (42 tests). This covers content, research, tracker, discoverability and silo checks.
- **`npm run release:check`:** passes.
- **Negative test:** removing one pillar link made `check:silo` fail on both the pillar and the orphaned spoke.

## Open items (not changed in this pass)

- **External citations:** 9 posts have 2 contextual external links. The SOP asks for at least 3, while CLAUDE.md allows 2 to 3. Adding a third needs a verified source per post: S7, S8, S11, S13, S14, S15, S16, S23, S24.
- **Staging robots.txt:** it allows crawling. The SOP says to disallow all, but staging already sends noindex in the meta tag and the X-Robots-Tag header. Blocking crawling would stop crawlers seeing that noindex, so it was left as is pending an owner decision.
- **Pillar size:** the Markets & Stocks pillar has 15 spokes against the SOP guide of 4 to 8. A later split, for example screeners into their own silo, would need new slugs and redirects.
- **Single-post silos:** Payments and Fintech have no spokes yet. These are gaps to fill next.
- **Answer-first rewrites:** the H2 answer-first and question-shaped heading pass (SOP step 6) is a content edit for each post and has not been done.
- **Off-site:** Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools and GA4 setup, and monthly AI-visibility checks (SOP step 8), are owner tasks. No GA4 tag is in the site template.
