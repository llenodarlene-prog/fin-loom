# FinLoom Brand

## Positioning

Data-first finance and fintech intelligence for readers who want to understand how money, markets, platforms, and investor behavior are changing.

## Promise

Follow the money, not the noise.

## Colors

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Violet | #2B1F5B | Navigation, major headings, footer, primary buttons |
| Royal Violet | #5A46D6 | Links, active states, charts, selected CTAs |
| Soft Violet | #A78BFA | Supporting highlights and chart accents |
| Light Neutral | #E9E7F4 | Cards, research panels, subtle section backgrounds |
| Charcoal | #0F1226 | Body copy, tables, captions |
| White | #FFFFFF | Primary page background |

## Typography

Libre Baskerville for display and editorial headings. Manrope for body, navigation, buttons, labels, and data tables.

## Visual Direction

Premium financial intelligence: modern architecture, market data, payments infrastructure, business environments, and human decision-making. Avoid glowing coins, generic crypto rockets, overloaded charts, Wall Street clichés, and excessive violet gradients.
