# Brand

Source: FinLoom - Website Branding Brief and the tracker's Brand Strategy sheet (`data/brand-strategy.json`).

- **Tagline:** Financial Insights. A Brighter Tomorrow.
- **Voice:** numerate, calm, market-literate, anti-hype. Separate measured facts from interpretation.
- **Palette:** Deep Violet #2B1F5B, Royal Violet #5A46D6, Soft Violet #A78BFA, Light Neutral #E9E7F4, Charcoal #0F1226. Mostly white and light neutral; violet is the anchor, not a gradient.
- **Type:** Libre Baskerville 400 for display and headings; Manrope 400 for body and 500 for navigation, buttons, and labels.
- **Imagery:** calm and credible. No glowing coins, rockets, overloaded charts, or neon.
- **Logo:** a fixed brand asset. Do not recreate it in type or CSS. Until approved files are supplied the header shows the site name as text.

The site should read as an intelligent financial publication, not a trading platform sales page.
