**Decision.** Build the expanded roadmap in eight sprints. Start with
stable, low-difficulty tools. Add regulated, live-data and
high-maintenance calculators only after their source-update process is
ready.

# Recommended first release

The first release should contain five tools that combine attainable
rankings, clear formulas and low maintenance:

- Salary to Hourly Calculator

- CAGR Calculator

- Profit Margin Calculator

- Mortgage Recast Calculator

- Coast FIRE Calculator

**Why these five.** They create new traffic paths without duplicating
FinLoom’s current savings, debt payoff, dividend and tax calculators.

# Expanded second set

**New priorities 21–40.** The expansion adds five opportunity groups:
housing and refinance, specialist vehicle and property lending,
retirement and benefit calculations, operator-finance tools, and
payments or crypto utilities. Sprint 5 should begin with Sales Tax, Home
Affordability, Boat Loan, Business Valuation and Car Refinance. The
sales-tax tool should launch with manual rate entry before FinLoom
commits to maintaining nationwide location data.

# Current coverage and gaps

FinLoom already lists 25 calculators. Coverage is strongest in savings,
debt payoff, investment growth, dividends and federal tax. The expanded
40-tool set fills ten clear gaps:

- Income and payroll conversions

- Housing decisions and mortgage structure

- Retirement milestones and mandatory distributions

- Loan origination and total borrowing cost

- Operator finance and business lending

- Options and more advanced market calculations

- Home affordability, equity and refinance decisions

- Federal, military and Social Security retirement estimates

- Payments, fee and currency utilities

- Crypto transaction-cost calculations

| **Existing cluster** | **Tools** | **Coverage assessment**                                                            |
|----------------------|-----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Money and Banking    | 11        | Strong in savings, credit utilization, debt payoff and mortgage payoff.            |
| Investing            | 8         | Strong in growth, dividends, withdrawals, DCA, fees and IRA comparisons.           |
| Tax                  | 4         | Focused on capital gains, retirement withdrawals, self-employment and 1099 income. |
| Markets and Stocks   | 2         | Limited to average cost and stock profit.                                          |

# Implementation briefs by sprint

## Sprint 1 Stable quick wins

Ship these first. Their formulas are stable and they extend existing
clusters without heavy regulatory maintenance.

### 1 Salary to Hourly Calculator

> **Search case:** 49,500 US searches, SD 14, CPC \$0.98. **Route:**
> Money and Banking \| /money-banking/salary-to-hourly-calculator/
> **Inputs:** Annual salary, hours per week, working weeks, paid time
> off. **Outputs:** Hourly, daily, weekly and monthly equivalents.
> **Method:** Annual salary / paid working hours. **Maintenance:**
> Stable. **Editorial value:** Connect to take-home pay and budgeting
> content.

### 2 CAGR Calculator

> **Search case:** 14,800 US searches, SD 20, CPC \$3.70. **Route:**
> Investing \| /investing/cagr-calculator/ **Inputs:** Starting value,
> ending value, years. **Outputs:** CAGR, total return and annualized
> value path. **Method:** (Ending / Starting)^(1 / years) - 1.
> **Maintenance:** Stable. **Editorial value:** Connect to investment
> growth and total return.

### 3 Profit Margin Calculator

> **Search case:** 22,200 US searches, SD 25, CPC \$4.11. **Route:**
> Fintech \| /fintech/profit-margin-calculator/ **Inputs:** Revenue,
> cost, price and units. **Outputs:** Profit, margin, markup and
> break-even price. **Method:** (Revenue - cost) / revenue.
> **Maintenance:** Stable. **Editorial value:** Open an operator-finance
> and unit-economics cluster.

### 4 Mortgage Recast Calculator

> **Search case:** 18,100 US searches, SD 19, CPC \$3.93. **Route:**
> Money and Banking \| /money-banking/mortgage-recast-calculator/
> **Inputs:** Balance, rate, remaining term, lump sum and fee.
> **Outputs:** New payment, monthly savings and interest difference.
> **Method:** Re-amortize remaining balance at unchanged rate and term.
> **Maintenance:** Stable. **Editorial value:** Extend mortgage payoff
> without duplicating it.

### 5 Coast FIRE Calculator

> **Search case:** 40,500 US searches, SD 14, CPC \$2.96. **Route:**
> Investing \| /investing/coast-fire-calculator/ **Inputs:** Age,
> retirement age, balance, spending, return, inflation, withdrawal rate.
> **Outputs:** Coast number, gap and projected retirement value.
> **Method:** FI target discounted by real return over remaining years.
> **Maintenance:** Annual assumptions review. **Editorial value:**
> Connect to withdrawal and retirement calculators.

# Shared product requirements

Every calculator should use the same FinLoom interaction model and
evidence standard:

- Calculate in the browser when possible and never require registration.

- Show formulas, assumptions, definitions and a dated methodology note.

- Keep inputs on the left and the main result, comparison and chart on
  the right.

- Update results as inputs change and preserve the last valid result
  after an error.

- Provide a detailed table, worked example, FAQs and contextual internal
  links.

- Offer shareable result text without storing or exposing personal
  financial inputs.

- Separate fixed formulas from changeable tax, program, rate and policy
  data.

- Use educational-estimate language and avoid recommendations or
  guaranteed outcomes.

# Release gates

- Math QA: test zero values, negative values, boundary values and known
  worked examples.

- Source QA: record the source URL, effective date and next review date
  for every changeable input.

- Content QA: explain what the result means, what it excludes and when
  professional advice may be needed.

- SEO QA: one primary intent per URL, unique title and description,
  indexable result explanation and internal links.

- Privacy QA: no server storage of personal inputs unless a future
  feature explicitly requires consent.

- Accessibility QA: labeled controls, keyboard support, readable charts
  and non-color error messages.
