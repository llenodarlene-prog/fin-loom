# Personal Loan Calculator: Payment, APR and Fees

This personal loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a fixed-rate personal loan and shows how an origination fee changes the real cost. It works out the cash you receive, the total cost of borrowing and the APR once the fee is included.

```calculator
personal-loan
```

## How Origination Fees Change the Cost

Some lenders take an origination fee out of the loan before paying you. You repay the full loan amount but receive less cash. On a $15,000 loan at 12% for 3 years with a 5% fee, the payment is about $498.21 a month, you receive $14,250, and the APR is about 15.61%. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why APR Is Higher Than the Rate

APR measures the cost of credit as a yearly rate, including certain fees. Because the fee reduces the cash you receive while the payments stay the same, the effective rate is higher than the stated interest rate. Comparing offers by APR gives a fairer picture than comparing interest rates alone.

## Borrowing Enough After the Fee

If you need a set amount of cash, you may need to borrow more to cover the fee. The calculator shows the loan size needed so that the cash received equals your original amount, though the larger loan carries a higher payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is a Personal Loan Payment Calculated?

The payment is figured on the full loan amount at the stated rate over the term. A $15,000 loan at 12% for 3 years costs about $498.21 a month.

### What Is an Origination Fee?

It is an upfront charge some lenders deduct from the loan proceeds. A 5% fee on $15,000 is $750, so you receive $14,250 but repay the full $15,000.

### Why Is My APR Higher Than My Interest Rate?

APR includes certain fees, such as an origination fee. Because the fee reduces the cash you receive, the effective yearly cost is higher than the stated rate.
