# Land Loan Calculator: Monthly Payment and Interest

This land loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a lot or acreage from the price, down payment, any seller credit and fees added to the loan. It shows the amount financed, total interest and how the payment changes with the loan term.

```calculator
land-loan
```

## How the Land Loan Payment Is Calculated

The amount financed is the price minus the down payment and any seller credit, plus fees added to the loan. An $80,000 parcel with $16,000 down and $1,500 of financed fees leaves $65,500 to finance. At 8% for 15 years, the payment is about $625.95 a month, with about $47,171.38 of interest. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Comparing Land Loan Offers

Down payments, rates and terms for land can differ from a typical home mortgage, and they vary by lender. Ask each lender for its requirements and enter those figures to compare offers.

## Costs Beyond the Loan

Property taxes, surveys, utility hookups, clearing and permits are not included unless you add them as fees. Planning for them avoids surprises after closing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Down Payment Does a Land Loan Need?

It varies by lender and by the type of land. Enter the down payment from your offer to see how it changes the amount financed and the monthly payment.

### Can I Finance Closing Costs on a Land Loan?

Some lenders allow it. Add any financed fees in the fees field, or leave it at 0 if you will pay them in cash at closing.

### Does the Payment Include Property Tax?

No. The payment covers principal and interest only. Property taxes and other ownership costs are paid separately.
