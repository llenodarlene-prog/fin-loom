# Mortgage Recast Calculator: New Payment After a Lump Sum

This mortgage recast calculator shows what happens when you pay a lump sum toward your loan's principal and the lender recalculates the payment. It estimates the new monthly payment, the interest saved over the rest of the loan, and how long the recast fee takes to pay back.

```calculator
mortgage-recast
```

## How a Mortgage Recast Works

In a recast, you make a large principal payment and the lender re-amortizes the lower balance over the same remaining term at the same rate. The payment drops because there is less to repay over the same time. A $300,000 balance at 6.5% with 25 years left has a principal and interest payment of about $2,025.62. After a $50,000 lump sum, the payment falls to about $1,688.02. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Recast vs Refinance

A recast keeps your existing loan, rate and term. A refinance replaces the loan with a new one, which can change the rate and term but usually comes with closing costs. The [mortgage refinance calculator](/money-banking/mortgage-refinance-calculator/) compares a new loan with your current one.

## Recast vs Extra Payments

Paying the same lump sum without a recast also saves interest, but the payment stays the same and the loan ends sooner. A recast keeps the original end date and lowers the monthly payment instead. The [mortgage payoff calculator](/money-banking/mortgage-payoff-calculator/) shows how extra payments shorten the loan.

## Questions to Ask Your Servicer

Not every loan can be recast, and terms vary by lender. Ask whether your loan qualifies, the minimum lump sum, the fee and how long processing takes. The calculator's results depend on those details.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Mortgage Recast?

A recast is when you pay a lump sum toward principal and the lender recalculates your payment over the remaining term at the same rate. The loan itself does not change.

### Does a Recast Lower My Interest Rate?

No. The rate and remaining term stay the same. The payment falls only because the balance is lower after the lump sum.

### Is a Recast Better Than a Refinance?

It depends. A recast keeps your rate and charges a servicer fee, while a refinance can change the rate but usually involves closing costs. Compare both with your own numbers.
