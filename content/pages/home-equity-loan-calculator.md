# Home Equity Loan Calculator: How Much Can You Borrow?

This home equity loan calculator estimates how much you may be able to borrow against your home and what the loan would cost each month. It uses your home value, mortgage balance and the lender's combined loan-to-value (CLTV) limit.

```calculator
home-equity-loan
```

## How Borrowing Limits Work

Lenders cap the total of your mortgage and the new loan at a share of the home's value, called the combined loan-to-value ratio. With a $450,000 home, a $250,000 mortgage and an 85% limit, total borrowing can reach $382,500, leaving about $132,500 available. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Estimating the Payment

A home equity loan is usually a lump sum repaid in fixed monthly payments. A $50,000 loan at 8.5% for 15 years costs about $492.37 a month, with about $38,626.56 of interest over the term.

## Your Home Secures the Loan

Because the home is collateral, missing payments puts it at risk. Closing costs and appraisal fees can also apply. Compare the full cost with other ways of borrowing before deciding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Equity Can I Borrow?

It depends on the lender's combined loan-to-value limit. Multiply your home value by that limit and subtract your mortgage balance to estimate the maximum.

### What Is Combined Loan-to-Value?

It is the total of all loans secured by the home divided by its value. A $300,000 total on a $450,000 home is a CLTV of 66.7%.

### Is a Home Equity Loan Fixed Rate?

Many home equity loans have a fixed rate and set payments, while lines of credit can have variable rates. Check the terms of any offer you receive.
