# Commercial Mortgage Calculator: Payment, DSCR and Balloon

This commercial mortgage calculator estimates the loan amount, payment and balloon on an income property, along with the debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) and debt yield that lenders use to size commercial loans.

```calculator

```

## How the Loan Is Sized

The loan equals the property value times the loan-to-value ratio. A 70% loan on a $2,000,000 property is $1,400,000. At 7% amortized over 25 years, the payment is about $9,894.91 a month, and a balloon of about $1,100,868 is due after a 10-year term. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## What DSCR Measures

DSCR divides net operating income by yearly debt payments. With $160,000 of income and about $118,739 of yearly payments, the DSCR is about 1.35, meaning income covers the payments 1.35 times. Lenders set their own minimum DSCR, along with limits on LTV and debt yield.

## Debt Yield and Cap Rate

Debt yield is net operating income divided by the loan, here about 11.43%. The cap rate on value is net operating income divided by the property value, here 8%. Both give a quick read on income relative to the loan and the price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Good DSCR?

Lenders set their own minimums, which vary by property type and market. A DSCR above 1.0 means income covers the debt payments.

### Why Do Commercial Mortgages Have Balloon Payments?

The payment is often based on a longer amortization than the loan term, so a balance remains at the end. That balance is due as a balloon.

### What Is Net Operating Income?

It is the property's income minus operating expenses such as taxes, insurance and maintenance, before debt payments and income taxes.
