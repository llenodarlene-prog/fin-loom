# Motorcycle Loan Calculator: Monthly Payment

This motorcycle loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a new or used bike from the price, down payment, trade-in, sales tax and fees. It shows the amount financed, total interest and the full purchase cost, with payments compared across terms.

```calculator
motorcycle-loan
```

## How the Motorcycle Payment Is Calculated

The amount financed is the price minus the down payment and trade-in, plus sales tax and fees added to the loan. A $15,000 motorcycle with $2,000 down, 6% sales tax and $300 of fees leaves $14,200 to finance. At 9% for 4 years, the payment is about $353.37 a month, with about $2,761.64 of interest. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Comparing Loan Terms

The table shows the payment and total interest for several terms. A shorter term raises the payment but cuts interest, which can count for more on a vehicle that loses value over time.

## Ownership Costs to Budget For

Gear, insurance, maintenance and registration are not included in the payment. Adding them gives a fuller view of the monthly cost of owning the bike.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Typical Motorcycle Loan Term?

Terms vary by lender and loan size. The calculator compares terms from two to six years so you can see how each changes the payment and interest.

### Is Sales Tax Included in the Loan?

It can be. Enter your state and local rate to finance the tax, or set it to 0 if you pay it separately at purchase.

### How Can I Lower My Motorcycle Payment?

A bigger down payment, a lower rate or a longer term lowers the payment. A longer term usually adds interest, so compare total cost too.
