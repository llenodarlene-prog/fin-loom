# Rental Property Calculator: Cash Flow, Cap Rate and Returns

This rental property calculator estimates whether a rental would bring in more than it costs. Enter the purchase price, down payment, mortgage terms, closing costs, rent and operating expenses. It returns monthly cash flow, net operating income, cap rate, cash-on-cash return and debt service coverage, with a line-by-line income and expense table.

```calculator
rental-property
```

## How the Rental Property Calculator Works

Net operating income (NOI) is rent after vacancy minus operating expenses such as taxes, insurance, HOA fees, maintenance and management. Mortgage payments are not operating expenses. Cash flow is NOI minus mortgage payments. Cap rate is NOI divided by the purchase price, and cash-on-cash return is yearly cash flow divided by the cash you put in.

With the defaults, a $300,000 property with 25% down at 7% and $2,600 monthly rent produces about $19,673 of NOI, a 6.56% cap rate. After a mortgage payment of about $1,497, cash flow is about $142 a month, a 2.04% cash-on-cash return on $84,000 invested. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Cap Rate Versus Cash-on-Cash Return

Cap rate ignores financing, so it compares properties on their own income. Cash-on-cash return includes the mortgage, so it shows the return on your own money. A property can have a solid cap rate but thin cash flow when the loan rate is high, as the default example shows.

## Stress-Testing the Numbers

Small changes in rent, vacancy or repairs can turn cash flow negative. Try a higher vacancy rate or maintenance reserve to see how much room the deal has. The [mortgage refinance calculator](/money-banking/mortgage-refinance-calculator/) can show how a lower rate later would change the payment.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Appreciation, depreciation, taxes on rental income, capital expenditures beyond the maintenance reserve and selling costs are not included. Inputs are held steady over time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is Rental Property Cash Flow Calculated?

Take rent after vacancy, subtract operating expenses to get net operating income, then subtract the mortgage payments. What remains is cash flow.

### What Is a Cap Rate?

It is net operating income divided by the purchase price. A property with $19,673 of NOI bought for $300,000 has a cap rate of about 6.56%.

### What Is Cash-on-Cash Return?

It is yearly cash flow divided by the cash you invested, including the down payment and closing costs. It shows the return on your own money.
