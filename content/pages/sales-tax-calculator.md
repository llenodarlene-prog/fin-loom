# Sales Tax Calculator: Add or Remove Sales Tax

This sales tax calculator adds sales tax to a price or takes it out of a total. Enter the amount and your combined state and local sales tax rate, and choose whether the amount is before or after tax. It returns the pre-tax price, the tax and the total, with a table for multiple quantities.

```calculator
sales-tax
```

## How the Sales Tax Calculator Works

To add tax, multiply the price by the rate: 8.25% of $100 is $8.25, for a $108.25 total. To remove tax from a total, divide by one plus the rate: $108.25 ÷ 1.0825 is $100 before tax. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Finding the Right Rate

Sales tax rates are set by states and often by counties, cities and special districts too, so the combined rate depends on where the sale takes place. Some items, such as certain groceries or medicines, may be exempt or taxed at a different rate. The calculator does not include built-in rates because they change and vary by address. Check your state revenue department or a recent receipt for the rate that applies.

## For Sellers

Businesses that collect sales tax can use the remove-tax mode to split a tax-inclusive price into the amount they keep and the tax they owe. Rules on what to collect and when to file vary by state.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Item-level exemptions, rounding rules and other taxes such as excise or use tax are not included.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do You Calculate Sales Tax?

Multiply the price by the sales tax rate as a decimal. At 8.25%, a $100 price has $8.25 of tax, for a total of $108.25.

### How Do I Find the Price Before Tax?

Divide the total by one plus the tax rate. A $108.25 total at 8.25% tax was $100 before tax.

### Why Does Sales Tax Vary by Location?

States, counties, cities and special districts can each add their own rate, so the combined rate depends on where the purchase is made.
