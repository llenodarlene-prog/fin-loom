# Boat Loan Calculator: Monthly Payment and Interest

This boat loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a boat purchase from the price, down payment, trade-in, sales tax and fees. It shows the amount financed, total interest and the full cost, with a table comparing loan terms.

```calculator
boat-loan
```

## How the Boat Payment Is Calculated

The amount financed is the price minus the down payment and any trade-in, plus sales tax and fees added to the loan. A $60,000 boat with $12,000 down, 6% sales tax and $500 of fees leaves $52,100 to finance. At 8% for 15 years, the payment is about $497.89 a month, with about $37,521.05 of interest. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why Long Boat Loan Terms Add Up

Boat loans can run for many years, which keeps payments low but raises total interest. In the example, interest over 15 years is well over half the amount financed. The term table shows how much a shorter term would save.

## Costs Beyond the Loan

Insurance, storage, maintenance, fuel and registration are not included. Adding them to the monthly payment gives a truer picture of what owning the boat will cost.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Long Can a Boat Loan Be?

Terms vary by lender, boat type and loan size. The calculator accepts terms up to 20 years so you can compare shorter and longer options.

### Is a Down Payment Required for a Boat Loan?

Many lenders ask for one, and the amount varies. A larger down payment lowers the amount financed, the monthly payment and the total interest.

### Does the Payment Include Insurance?

No. The payment covers principal and interest only. Insurance, storage, maintenance and other ownership costs are extra.
