# About FinLoom

FinLoom is a data-first finance and fintech publication. Our editorial approach is clear, numerate, calm, market-literate, and anti-hype.

## Editorial Method

We prefer regulators, official datasets, filings, company primary sources, and recognized research. Important figures should carry enough date, population, definition, and source context to be interpreted correctly.
