# Student Loan Payoff Calculator: Payment, Interest and Extra Payments

This student loan payoff calculator shows your scheduled monthly payment and total interest, then tests how much sooner an extra monthly payment could clear the loan and how much interest it could save. Enter your balance, interest rate, repayment term and any extra amount.

```calculator
student-loan
```

## How the Student Loan Payoff Calculator Works

The scheduled payment pays the loan off in equal monthly amounts over the term. Each month, interest is the balance times the rate divided by 12, and the rest of the payment reduces the balance. With the defaults, $30,000 at 6.5% over 10 years means a payment of about $340.64 and about $10,877 of interest. Adding $100 a month could finish nearly 3 years sooner and save about $3,347. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Making Extra Payments Count

Extra payments only shorten the loan if they go to principal. Some servicers apply extra money to future payments instead. Ask your servicer how to direct extra payments to principal, and check that your monthly statement shows the balance falling faster.

When you have several loans, putting extra money toward the highest-rate loan first usually saves the most interest. The [debt snowball calculator](/money-banking/debt-snowball-calculator/) compares that approach with paying the smallest balance first.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Federal income-driven repayment plans, forgiveness programs, deferment, forbearance and variable rates are not modeled. Those can change your payment or total cost a great deal, so check your options with your servicer before changing your plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is a Student Loan Payment Calculated?

The payment is set so equal monthly amounts pay off the balance and interest over the term. Monthly interest is the balance times the annual rate divided by 12.

### Does Paying Extra on Student Loans Save Money?

Yes, if the extra goes to principal. It lowers the balance that interest is charged on, which shortens the loan and cuts total interest.

### Does This Calculator Include Income-Driven Repayment?

No. It models a standard fixed payment over the term you choose. Income-driven plans base payments on income and family size instead.
