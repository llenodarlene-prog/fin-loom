# Pension Calculator: Monthly Pension vs Lump Sum

This pension calculator compares a monthly pension with a lump-sum offer. It finds what the pension stream is worth today at a return you choose, with an optional yearly increase, and the return the lump sum would need to earn to pay the same amounts.

```calculator
pension
```

## How the Comparison Works

The calculator discounts each monthly payment back to today at your chosen return. A $2,500 monthly pension for 25 years is worth about $432,423 today at a 5% return, more than a $400,000 lump sum. The lump sum would need to earn about 5.83% a year to pay the same amounts. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why Life Expectancy Counts

A pension pays for as long as the terms say, often for life, while a lump sum can run out. The number of years you enter has a large effect on the result. Trying several values shows how the answer changes if you live longer or shorter than expected.

## Other Factors to Weigh

Survivor benefits, cost-of-living adjustments, taxes, the plan's financial strength and your other income all affect the choice. A lump sum offers flexibility and can be left to heirs, while a pension removes investment risk. The calculator compares value only.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Should I Take a Pension or a Lump Sum?

It depends on your health, other income, investment comfort and the offer terms. The calculator compares present values, which is one input among several.

### What Is the Break-Even Return?

It is the yearly return the lump sum would need to earn to pay the same monthly amounts for the years you enter. A higher break-even favors the pension.

### Does the Calculator Include Taxes?

No. Pension payments and lump-sum withdrawals can be taxed differently, and rollover options vary. Check the tax treatment of your plan's choices.
