# Mobile Home Loan Calculator: Monthly Payment

This mobile home loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a manufactured or mobile home from the price, down payment, any credit, sales tax and fees added to the loan. It shows the amount financed, total interest and a comparison of loan terms.

```calculator
mobile-home-loan
```

## How the Payment Is Calculated

The amount financed is the price minus the down payment and any credit, plus sales tax and fees added to the loan. A $90,000 home with $9,000 down and $1,500 of financed fees leaves $82,500 to finance. At 8.5% for 20 years, the payment is about $715.95 a month, with about $89,329 of interest. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why the Term Counts So Much

On a long loan, interest can rival the price of the home. In the example, total interest is more than the amount financed. The term table shows how a shorter term changes both the payment and the interest.

## Costs the Payment Leaves Out

Lot rent, property taxes, insurance, utilities and setup costs are not included unless you add them as fees. Including them gives a fuller monthly budget.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Are Mobile Home Loans Different?

Loan types, rates and terms vary by lender and by how the home is set up and titled. Ask each lender what applies to your home before comparing offers.

### Is Sales Tax Charged on a Mobile Home?

Rules vary by state and by whether the home is new or used. Enter your rate if tax applies and will be financed, or leave it at 0.

### Does the Payment Include Lot Rent?

No. The payment covers principal and interest on the loan only. Lot rent, taxes and insurance are separate monthly costs.
