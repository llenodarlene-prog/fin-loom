# Follow the Money, Not the Noise.

FinLoom is an independent financial publication built for readers who want the economics underneath the headline. We connect market behavior, investor decisions, financial infrastructure, and business models with evidence that can be checked.

## Explore FinLoom

Start with [Money & Banking](/money-banking/), [Investing](/investing/), [Markets & Stocks](/markets-stocks/), [Fintech](/fintech/), [Payments](/payments/), or [Crypto & Digital Assets](/crypto/).

## How We Work

We prioritize primary sources, define important measures, separate observed data from forecasts, and recheck fast-changing facts before publication.
