# Balance Transfer Calculator: Fees, Promo APR and Savings

This balance transfer calculator compares keeping a card balance where it is with moving it to a card offering a promotional rate. Enter the balance, your current APR, your monthly payment, the transfer fee, the promotional APR and how long it lasts, and the rate afterward. It shows the interest and fees for each option, how long each takes to pay off and the payment needed to clear the balance before the promotion ends.

```calculator
balance-transfer
```

## How Balance Transfers Work

The [CFPB notes](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-do-i-need-to-know-if-im-thinking-about-consolidating-my-credit-card-debt-en-1861/) that many card companies offer zero-percent or low-interest balance transfers, that the promotional rate usually lasts a limited time, and that the rate can rise afterward. You will probably also pay a balance transfer fee.

## How the Balance Transfer Calculator Works

The fee is added to the balance when it moves. Each month, both options charge interest at their APR and apply the same payment. With the defaults, moving $6,000 with a 3% fee to 0% for 15 months, then 24%, while paying $300 a month, costs about $300 in fees and interest against about $1,739 if you stay, a saving of about $1,440. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

The calculator also shows the balance left when the promotion ends, $1,680 in the default case, and the payment needed to clear it in time, about $412 a month.

## When a Transfer Might Not Help

A transfer helps most when you can pay down a large share of the balance during the promotion. If new purchases build up on either card, or payments are late, the savings can disappear. Some cards end a promotional rate after a late payment, so check the terms.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

New purchases, minimum fee floors, late fees and penalty rates are not modeled. Interest is calculated monthly rather than on a daily balance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Balance Transfer Worth It?

It can be when the fee is smaller than the interest you would save and you can pay down most of the balance before the promotional rate ends.

### How Is a Balance Transfer Fee Charged?

Usually as a percentage of the amount moved, added to the new balance. Some cards charge a flat minimum instead when it is higher.

### What Happens When the Promotion Ends?

Any remaining balance starts accruing interest at the card's regular rate, which can be much higher. The calculator shows what would be left at that point.
