# Mortgage Refinance Calculator: Payment, Savings and Break-Even

This mortgage refinance calculator compares your current loan with a new one. Enter your balance, current rate and years left, then the new rate, term and closing costs. It shows both principal and interest payments, the monthly savings, how many months it would take to recover the closing costs and how total payments change over the life of each loan.

```calculator
refinance
```

## How the Mortgage Refinance Calculator Works

Each payment uses the standard amortization formula for the balance, rate and term. Break-even months equal upfront closing costs divided by monthly savings. With the defaults, $300,000 at 7% with 27 years left costs about $2,063 a month. A new 30-year loan at 6% costs about $1,799, saving about $265 a month, so $6,000 of closing costs are recovered in about 23 months. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Payment Savings Versus Total Cost

Starting a new 30-year term can lower the payment even when total interest rises, because the loan runs longer. The calculator shows both views. If you plan to sell or refinance again before the break-even point, the closing costs may not pay off.

## Points and Lender Credits

The [CFPB explains](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-should-i-use-lender-credits-and-points-also-called-discount-points-en-136/) that points lower your interest rate in exchange for paying more at closing, while lender credits lower closing costs in exchange for a higher rate. It suggests asking a loan officer for options with and without points over different timeframes. Enter each option's rate and costs to compare them here.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Property taxes, insurance, mortgage insurance and escrow are not included, nor are cash-out amounts. Rolled-in closing costs are added to the new balance and repaid with interest.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do You Calculate a Refinance Break-Even Point?

Divide the upfront closing costs by the monthly payment savings. Costs of $6,000 and savings of $265 a month break even in about 23 months.

### Can Refinancing Lower My Payment but Cost More?

Yes. A longer new term can reduce the monthly payment while increasing the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

### Should I Roll Closing Costs Into the Loan?

It avoids paying cash upfront, but the costs are added to the balance and repaid with interest, which raises the total cost of the loan.
