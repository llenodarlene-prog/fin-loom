# Crypto Profit Calculator: Gain, Fees and Break-Even Price

This crypto profit calculator works out the result of a cryptocurrency trade after fees. Enter how much you invested, the buy and sell prices per coin and your exchange's fee rates. It returns the profit or loss, the percentage return, the coins bought, the proceeds and the sell price needed to break even once fees are counted.

```calculator
crypto-profit
```

## How the Crypto Profit Calculator Works

The buy fee comes off the amount invested, so coins bought = (amount − buy fee) ÷ buy price. When you sell, proceeds = coins × sell price − sell fee. Profit is proceeds minus the original amount. With the defaults, $1,000 at $50,000 with 0.5% fees each way buys 0.0199 coins; selling at $60,000 returns about $1,188, a profit of about $188, or 18.8%. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why Fees Raise the Break-Even Price

Because fees are paid on both the purchase and the sale, the price has to rise just to get back to even. In the default case, the coin must reach about $50,504, roughly 1% above the purchase price. Spreads, which some platforms build into the price, raise it further.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Network fees, withdrawal fees and spreads are not included unless you add them to the fee rates. Taxes on gains are not included.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do You Calculate Crypto Profit?

Subtract the buy fee, divide by the buy price to get coins, multiply by the sell price, subtract the sell fee, then subtract what you invested.

### What Is a Break-Even Price in Crypto?

It is the sell price at which proceeds after fees equal the amount you invested. Fees on both trades push it above your purchase price.

### Does the Calculator Include Taxes?

No. It shows the result before taxes. Gains and losses on crypto can have tax consequences, so check current rules for your situation.
