# Rent vs Buy Calculator: Which Costs Less Over Time?

This rent vs buy calculator compares the net cost of renting with the net cost of buying over the years you expect to stay. It accounts for rent increases, mortgage payments, taxes, maintenance, home price changes, selling costs and what your down payment could earn if invested instead.

```calculator
rent-vs-buy
```

## How the Comparison Works

The net cost of buying is the down payment plus all mortgage, tax, insurance and maintenance payments, minus the equity you keep after selling. The net cost of renting is all rent paid, minus the growth your down payment could have earned. With the default inputs over 7 years, renting costs about $169,721 and buying about $142,044, so buying comes out about $27,677 ahead. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why Time Counts Most

Buying has large upfront and exit costs, such as the down payment and selling costs. The longer you stay, the more time equity and price growth have to offset them. Try shorter and longer stays to see where the answer changes.

## The Assumptions Drive the Result

Home price growth and investment returns are guesses about the future, and small changes can flip the outcome. Treat the result as a way to test scenarios, not a prediction. Closing costs at purchase and tax effects are not included.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is It Cheaper to Rent or Buy?

It depends on how long you stay, rent levels, home prices, rates and future growth. The calculator compares net costs under the assumptions you enter.

### Why Include What the Down Payment Could Earn?

Money used for a down payment cannot be invested elsewhere. Counting that lost growth makes the comparison fairer to renting.

### How Long Do I Need to Stay for Buying to Pay Off?

There is no single answer. Run the calculator with different stay lengths to see when buying overtakes renting under your assumptions.
