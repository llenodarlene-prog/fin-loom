# Budget Planner and Expense Tracker: Budget vs Actual

This budget planner lets you set a monthly budget for nine common categories and track what you actually spent against it. Enter your take-home income, a budget and an actual amount for each category. It shows the difference for each line, which categories are over budget, how much income is left and your savings rate, and you can download the result as a CSV file.

```calculator
budget-planner
```

## How the Budget Planner Works

For each category, the difference is budget minus actual, so a negative number means you overspent. Totals compare all budgets with all spending, and income left is take-home pay minus total spending. The savings rate adds what you put into savings to any income left unspent, then divides by income.

With the defaults, $5,000 of income, $4,700 budgeted and $4,730 spent leaves $270, with three categories over budget. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Using Budget Versus Actual

A budget is a plan; actual spending shows how the month really went. Comparing the two each month helps spot categories that regularly run over, which may need a bigger budget or a spending change. The [CFPB's guide to building an emergency fund](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/) notes that tracking the timing of money coming in and going out can reveal chances to save.

Once you know what is left each month, the [financial goal planner](/money-banking/financial-goal-planner/) can turn it into deposits toward specific goals.

## Your Data Stays on Your Device

The planner runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is stored or sent, and the CSV file is created on your device. No bank connection is needed.

## What the Planner Leaves Out

It covers one month at a time and does not import transactions or carry balances forward. Irregular yearly costs, such as insurance premiums, work best entered as a monthly share.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Make a Monthly Budget?

Start with take-home income, set an amount for each spending category, then track what you actually spend and adjust categories that regularly run over.

### What Is a Savings Rate?

It is the share of income you save. Here it is your savings deposits plus any income left unspent, divided by take-home income.

### Is My Budget Data Saved?

No. The planner runs in your browser and does not store or send anything. Use the CSV download to keep a copy on your device.
