# Mortgage Calculator: Monthly Payment With Taxes and PMI

This mortgage calculator estimates your full monthly house payment, not just principal and interest. It adds property tax, homeowners insurance, HOA dues and private mortgage insurance (PMI) when the down payment is under 20%, and shows the total interest over the loan.

```calculator
mortgage
```

## How the Mortgage Payment Is Calculated

Principal and interest come from the standard amortization formula. A $400,000 home with 10% down is a $360,000 loan. At 6.5% for 30 years, principal and interest is about $2,275.44 a month. Adding 1.1% property tax, $1,800 a year of insurance and 0.5% PMI brings the total to about $2,942.11. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## What PITI Means

Lenders often describe a house payment as PITI: principal, interest, taxes and insurance. Many loans collect taxes and insurance through an escrow account each month, so the payment you make is closer to the total than to principal and interest alone.

## How PMI Works

Conventional loans with less than 20% down may require PMI. The calculator applies the PMI rate you enter while the down payment is under 20%. Your lender quotes the actual rate, and the loan's terms explain when PMI can be removed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Is the Payment on a $400,000 House?

With 10% down at 6.5% for 30 years, principal and interest is about $2,275 a month. Taxes, insurance and PMI can add several hundred dollars more.

### Does a Mortgage Payment Include Taxes?

It can. Many lenders collect property tax and insurance monthly in an escrow account. Enter your tax rate and insurance cost to see the full payment.

### When Is PMI Required?

PMI may be required on conventional loans with less than 20% down. Enter your lender's PMI rate, which the calculator applies until the down payment reaches 20%.
