# Financial Goal Planner: Monthly Savings for Each Goal

This financial goal planner works out how much you need to save each month to reach up to four goals on time. Give each goal a name, a target, months until the deadline, what you have saved and what you plan to deposit. The planner shows the required monthly deposit for each goal, your progress and whether each one is on track.

```calculator
goal-planner
```

## How the Financial Goal Planner Works

For each goal, the planner grows what you have already saved at the savings APY until the deadline, then works out the monthly deposit needed to cover the rest. With the defaults, a $15,000 emergency fund in 24 months with $5,000 saved and a $40,000 down payment in 60 months with $8,000 saved need about $868 a month together at a 3% APY. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

If your planned deposit is lower, the table shows how far short each goal would fall by its deadline.

## Setting Goals You Can Keep

The [CFPB's guide to building an emergency fund](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/an-essential-guide-to-building-an-emergency-fund/) notes that a specific savings goal can help you stay motivated. Breaking a large target into a monthly amount makes it easier to see whether a deadline is realistic, or whether to extend it.

When goals compete, some people fund the most urgent one first, then shift deposits once it is reached. The [emergency fund calculator](/money-banking/emergency-fund-calculator/) can help size the first goal on many lists.

## What the Planner Leaves Out

The APY is held steady and applied to every goal. Taxes, fees, inflation and changes to targets are not included. Goal names stay in your browser and are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Should I Save Each Month for a Goal?

Subtract what your current savings could grow to by the deadline from the target, then divide by the growth of a monthly deposit over that time.

### What If I Cannot Reach Every Goal?

Extend a deadline, lower a target or fund goals in order of priority. The planner shows the monthly amount each change would require.

### Are My Goal Names Saved?

No. Goal names and amounts stay in your browser while you use the page. Fin Loom does not store or send them anywhere.
