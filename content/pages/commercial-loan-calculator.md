# Commercial Loan Calculator: Payment and Balloon

This commercial loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a business or commercial real estate loan and the balloon payment due when the term is shorter than the amortization period.

```calculator
commercial-loan
```

## Amortization Period vs Loan Term

The amortization period sets the payment, as if the loan were repaid over that many years. The term is when the loan actually comes due. When the term is shorter, the remaining balance is due as a balloon. A $1,000,000 loan at 7.5% amortized over 25 years has a payment of about $7,389.91 a month. After 10 years, a balloon of about $797,175.11 is due. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Planning for the Balloon

Borrowers can repay a balloon by refinancing, selling the property or paying cash. Rates and lending conditions at that time may differ, so it helps to plan well before the term ends.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Origination fees, prepayment penalties, variable rates and lender requirements such as debt service coverage are not modeled. Use the results to compare structures, then confirm details with lenders.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Balloon Payment?

It is the remaining loan balance due in one payment at the end of the term, when the payment was set on a longer amortization schedule.

### Why Are Commercial Loans Amortized Longer Than the Term?

A longer amortization lowers the monthly payment, while a shorter term limits how long the lender commits. The balloon covers the difference at the end.

### How Is the Payment Calculated?

The payment uses the standard amortization formula over the amortization period, not the term. The balloon is the balance left after the term's payments.
