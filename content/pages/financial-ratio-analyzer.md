# Financial Ratio Analyzer: Liquidity, Leverage and Profitability

This financial ratio analyzer turns figures from a company's financial statements into eleven standard ratios. Enter balance sheet and income statement amounts from the same period, and it calculates liquidity ratios such as the current and quick ratio, leverage ratios such as debt-to-equity and interest coverage, profitability ratios such as margins and return on equity, and asset turnover.

```calculator
financial-ratios
```

## How the Financial Ratio Analyzer Works

Each ratio divides one reported figure by another, and the table lists every formula. With the defaults, $500,000 of current assets against $250,000 of current liabilities gives a current ratio of 2.0. Operating income of $240,000 on $2,000,000 of revenue is a 12% operating margin, and net income of $160,000 on $800,000 of equity is a 20% return on equity. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

Where a ratio needs a blank or zero denominator, it shows "Not available" instead of a misleading number.

## What the Ratio Groups Tell You

Liquidity ratios show whether short-term assets cover short-term obligations. Leverage ratios show how much of the business is financed by liabilities and how easily operating profit covers interest. Profitability ratios show how much of each sales dollar becomes profit and how efficiently equity and assets produce it.

## Comparing Ratios Fairly

Normal ranges differ a lot by industry. A software company and a grocery chain can have very different margins and inventory levels for sound reasons. Ratios are most useful when compared with the same company over several years and with close peers. Definitions also vary: some analysts use only interest-bearing debt in debt-to-equity, or average balances rather than period-end figures.

## What the Analyzer Leaves Out

It does not fetch company data, so enter figures from the filings yourself. One-time items, accounting changes and off-balance-sheet obligations can distort ratios.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Good Current Ratio?

It depends on the industry. A ratio above 1 means current assets exceed current liabilities, but compare with peers and the company's own history.

### How Is Return on Equity Calculated?

Divide net income by shareholders' equity. Net income of $160,000 on $800,000 of equity gives a return on equity of 20%.

### Why Do Ratio Definitions Differ Between Sources?

Analysts make different choices, such as using only interest-bearing debt or average balances. Check which definition a source uses before comparing figures.
