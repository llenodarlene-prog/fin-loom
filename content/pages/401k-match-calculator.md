# 401(k) Match Calculator: How Much Your Employer Adds

This 401(k) match calculator shows how much your employer could add to your plan this year. Enter your salary, the percentage you contribute and your employer's match formula, and it returns the match you earn, the maximum available, any match left unclaimed and your 2026 contribution limit.

```calculator
401k-match
```

## How the 401(k) Match Calculator Works

Most match formulas have two parts: a match rate and a cap. A common example is a 50% match on contributions up to 6% of salary. The employer adds 50 cents for each dollar you contribute, but only on contributions up to 6% of your pay.

With the defaults, a $75,000 salary and a 4% contribution, you put in $3,000. The employer matches 50% of that, $1,500. Contributing 6% would earn the full $2,250 match, so $750 a year is left unclaimed. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## The 2026 Contribution Limits

The [IRS announced](https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/401k-limit-increases-to-24500-for-2026-ira-limit-increases-to-7500) that the 2026 limit on employee contributions to 401(k), 403(b) and most 457 plans is $24,500, up from $23,500 for 2025. Employees aged 50 and over can add a catch-up contribution of $8,000. The calculator caps your contribution at those amounts.

The [IRS also sets an overall limit](https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-401k-and-profit-sharing-plan-contribution-limits) on total contributions from you and your employer: the lesser of 100% of pay or $72,000 in 2026, before catch-up contributions. That limit rarely applies to typical salaries and is not modeled.

## Why the Match Is Worth Checking

FINRA's guide to [getting started as an investor](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/getting-started-investing) notes that employers may offer matching contributions, which it calls essentially free money. Contributing at least enough to earn the full match is a common first goal, though the right amount depends on your budget and other needs.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Plans differ. Some use tiered formulas, such as 100% of the first 3% and 50% of the next 2%. Many have vesting schedules, so matched money may not be fully yours until you have worked there a set time. Some plans match per paycheck and others true up at year end. Investment growth, taxes and the higher catch-up for ages 60 to 63 are not modeled.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Does a 401(k) Match Work?

Your employer adds money based on what you contribute, using a match rate and a cap. A 50% match up to 6% of salary adds 50 cents per dollar on the first 6%.

### What Is the 401(k) Limit for 2026?

The IRS set the 2026 employee contribution limit at $24,500. Employees aged 50 and over can add an $8,000 catch-up contribution, for $32,500 in total.

### Do Employer Matches Count Toward My Limit?

No. Employer matches do not count toward your $24,500 employee limit, but they do count toward the overall limit on total contributions, which is $72,000 in 2026.
