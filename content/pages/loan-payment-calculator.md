# Loan Payment Calculator: Monthly Payment and Interest

This loan payment calculator works out the monthly payment for a fixed-rate loan, along with the total interest and how the balance falls each year. It works for car loans, personal loans, student loans and other installment debt with a fixed rate and term.

```calculator
loan-payment
```

## How the Monthly Payment Is Calculated

A fixed-rate loan payment uses the standard amortization formula: payment = P × i ÷ (1 − (1 + i)^−n), where P is the amount borrowed, i is the annual rate divided by 12, and n is the number of monthly payments. A $25,000 loan at 8% for 5 years costs about $506.91 a month and $5,414.59 in total interest. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## How Term and Rate Change the Cost

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but adds interest, because the balance is repaid more slowly. A higher rate raises both. Running the same amount at two terms shows the trade-off clearly. The yearly table in the calculator shows how much principal and interest you will have paid by the end of each year.

## Interest Rate vs APR

The calculator uses the interest rate. A loan's annual percentage rate (APR) can be higher when fees are charged. For loans with an origination fee, the [personal loan calculator](/money-banking/personal-loan-calculator/) estimates the APR from the fee and payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is a Monthly Loan Payment Calculated?

The payment equals P × i ÷ (1 − (1 + i)^−n), where P is the loan amount, i is the monthly rate and n is the number of payments. The calculator does this math for you.

### Does a Longer Loan Term Save Money?

No. A longer term lowers each payment but usually raises total interest, because you borrow the money for longer. A shorter term costs more monthly and less overall.

### Does This Include Fees or Insurance?

No. The payment covers principal and interest only. Fees, insurance and taxes are not included, and fees can make the APR higher than the interest rate.
