# Net Worth Calculator: Assets, Debts and 2025 Benchmarks

This net worth calculator adds up what you own and subtracts what you owe. Enter your assets and debts by category to see your net worth, your home equity and the share of your assets financed by debt, alongside the Federal Reserve's 2025 median and mean for families in your age group.

```calculator
net-worth
```

## How the Net Worth Calculator Works

Net worth is total assets minus total debts. With the defaults, assets of $463,000 and debts of $310,000 give a net worth of $153,000. Home equity is the home's value minus the mortgage, here $70,000. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

Enter the home at its estimated market value and the mortgage as a separate debt. Entering home equity as an asset and the mortgage as a debt would subtract the mortgage twice.

## How Your Figure Compares

The comparison uses the Federal Reserve's [Changes in U.S. Family Finances from 2022 to 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/files/scf26.pdf), from the 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances released October 9, 2026. Median family net worth was $215,900 and the mean about $1.24 million, in 2025 dollars.

The median is the middle family, so it is a better guide to a typical household than the mean, which a few very wealthy families pull upward. These figures describe families, not individuals, and they are not targets.

## Why Net Worth Is Only Part of the Picture

Net worth measures accumulated wealth, not cash on hand. Retirement accounts may carry taxes or penalties on early withdrawal, and home equity can only be spent by selling or borrowing. The calculator shows cash and taxable investments separately for that reason. FinLoom's guide to [saving vs investing](/money-banking/saving-vs-investing/) explains how short-term and long-term money differ.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Values are pre-tax and reflect the date you enter them. Estimated taxes on retirement withdrawals, selling costs for a home and the value of pensions are not included.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do You Calculate Net Worth?

Add the value of everything you own, such as cash, investments, retirement accounts, your home and vehicles, then subtract everything you owe, such as mortgages, loans and card balances.

### What Is the Median Net Worth in 2025?

The Federal Reserve's 2025 Survey of Consumer Finances put median family net worth at $215,900, in 2025 dollars. The mean was about $1.24 million.

### Should I Include My Home in Net Worth?

Yes. Count the home's estimated market value as an asset and the mortgage balance as a debt. The difference is your home equity.
