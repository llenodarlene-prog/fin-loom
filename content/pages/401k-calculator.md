# 401k Calculator: Project Your Retirement Balance

This 401(k) calculator projects your balance at retirement from your contributions, your employer's match, salary increases and an assumed investment return. It breaks the result into what you put in, what your employer added and investment growth, with a balance table by age.

## How the Projection Works

Each year, your contribution and the employer match are added monthly and the balance grows at a steady return. A 30-year-old earning $70,000 who saves 8% with a 50% match on up to 6% of pay, 3% raises and a 6% return could reach about $1,438,287 by 65. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown, not a forecast.

## How the Employer Match Works

A match is often a percentage of your contribution up to a share of pay. A 50% match on up to 6% means your employer adds 3% of salary if you contribute at least 6%. Contributing below the match limit leaves part of it unclaimed.

## Limits and Taxes

The IRS sets yearly contribution limits, and plans may have vesting schedules and fees. The calculator does not apply them, so check your plan documents and the current IRS limits. Results are in future dollars and do not adjust for inflation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Will My 401k Be Worth at Retirement?

It depends on contributions, match, raises, return and time. Enter your figures to see a projection, which is an estimate rather than a forecast.

### How Does a 401k Match Work?

Your employer adds money based on what you contribute, often a share of your contribution up to a percentage of salary. The calculator lets you set both.

### Does the Calculator Apply IRS Limits?

No. It does not cap contributions at IRS limits or include catch-up contributions. Check the current IRS limits and your plan rules for your situation.
