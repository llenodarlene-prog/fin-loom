# Auto Loan Calculator: Monthly Car Payment

This auto loan calculator estimates your monthly car payment from the vehicle price, down payment, trade-in, sales tax and fees. It shows the amount financed, total interest and the full cost of the purchase, and compares payments across common loan terms.

```calculator
auto-loan
```

## How the Car Payment Is Calculated

The calculator first works out the amount financed: price minus down payment and trade-in, plus sales tax and any fees added to the loan. A $35,000 car with $5,000 down, a $3,000 trade-in, 6% sales tax on the price after trade-in and $500 of fees leaves $29,420 to finance. At 7% for 5 years, the payment is about $582.55 a month, with about $5,533.08 of interest. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## How Loan Term Changes the Cost

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but adds interest. The table in the calculator shows the payment and total interest for several terms at your rate, so you can see the trade-off before choosing.

## Sales Tax and Trade-Ins

This calculator taxes the price after subtracting the trade-in. Trade-in tax treatment varies by state, so check your state rules or the dealer worksheet. Set the tax rate to 0 if you plan to pay tax separately.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Much Car Payment Can I Afford?

It depends on your income, other debts and budget. The debt-to-income calculator shows how a new payment would change your ratio before you apply.

### Is Sales Tax Included in a Car Loan?

It can be, if the tax is financed with the vehicle. Enter your rate to include it, or set it to 0 if you pay the tax separately at purchase.

### Does a Bigger Down Payment Lower Interest?

Yes. A larger down payment reduces the amount financed, which lowers both the monthly payment and the total interest paid over the loan.
