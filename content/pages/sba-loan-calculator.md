# SBA Loan Calculator: Monthly Payment and Total Cost

This SBA loan calculator estimates the monthly payment on a small business loan backed by the U.S. Small Business Administration. Enter the amount, rate, term and any fees your lender adds to the loan to see the payment and total borrowing cost.

```calculator
sba-loan
```

## How the Payment Is Calculated

The calculator adds any financed guarantee fee and other fees to the loan amount, then uses the standard amortization formula. A $250,000 loan with a 2% financed fee and $2,500 of other fees at 10% for 10 years costs about $3,402.88 a month, with about $158,346 of interest and fees in total. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Fees and Rates Come From Your Offer

SBA program rules, fee levels and rate limits are set by the SBA and applied by lenders, and they can change. Rather than build in program figures, the calculator uses the rate and fees in your loan offer, so the result matches your actual terms.

## Comparing Loan Offers

Run each offer with its own rate, term and fees. A lower monthly payment from a longer term can still cost more overall, so compare total borrowing cost as well as the payment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is an SBA Loan Payment Calculated?

It uses the standard amortization formula on the loan amount plus any financed fees, over the term in months, at the rate in your offer.

### Can the Guarantee Fee Be Financed?

Some borrowers add it to the loan, while others pay it upfront. Enter the fee to finance it, or use 0 if you will pay it separately.

### Are SBA Loan Rates Fixed?

They can be fixed or variable, depending on the loan and lender. This calculator assumes a fixed rate, so use it as an estimate for variable loans.
