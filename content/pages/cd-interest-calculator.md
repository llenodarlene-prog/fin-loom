# CD Interest Calculator: Earnings at Maturity and Early Withdrawal

This CD interest calculator shows what a certificate of deposit could earn. Enter your deposit, the APY and the term, and it returns the interest earned and the balance at maturity. Under advanced options, you can test what withdrawing early would cost under a penalty measured in months of interest.

```calculator
cd-interest
```

## How the CD Interest Calculator Works

The annual percentage yield already includes compounding, so the calculator grows the deposit as deposit × (1 + APY) raised to the term in years. With the defaults, $10,000 at 4% APY for 12 months earns $400, for $10,400 at maturity. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

The [CFPB explains](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-certificate-of-deposit-cd-en-917/) that with a CD you generally agree to leave your money in place for a set term, and withdrawing early means paying a penalty. It suggests comparing the term, the rate and the penalty when you shop.

## What an Early Withdrawal Could Cost

Many banks set the penalty as a number of months of interest. In the calculator, withdrawing at month 6 from a 12-month, $10,000 CD at 4% APY earns about $198.04 by then. A three-month interest penalty costs about $98.53, leaving about $99.50 in net interest. If you withdraw very early, the penalty can exceed the interest earned and reduce your deposit.

Penalty rules vary by bank and term, so check your CD agreement. The calculator models one common approach, not every bank's method.

## CDs and Deposit Insurance

The CFPB notes that CDs at banks are insured up to $250,000 by the FDIC and CDs at credit unions up to $250,000 by the NCUA. Accounts in the same ownership category at one institution are added together toward that limit. FinLoom's comparison of [money market vs CD](/money-banking/money-market-vs-cd/) covers when a CD's fixed term makes sense.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Taxes on interest are not included. The rate is assumed fixed for the full term, with interest left in the CD. Renewal at maturity, which can happen automatically at a new rate, is not modeled.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Is CD Interest Calculated?

Using the APY, the balance after t years is the deposit times (1 + APY) to the power t. A $10,000 CD at 4% APY earns $400 over one year.

### What Is a CD Early Withdrawal Penalty?

It is a fee for taking money out before the term ends. Many banks charge a set number of months of interest, but the rules vary, so check your agreement.

### Can a CD Penalty Reduce My Deposit?

Yes. If you withdraw early enough, the penalty can be larger than the interest earned so far, so you could receive less than you deposited.
