# Crypto Allocation Calculator: Share of Portfolio and Risk

This crypto allocation calculator shows how much of your total portfolio is in cryptocurrency and what that means for risk. Enter the value of your crypto and your other investments, a target share you choose, and a price fall to test. It returns your current crypto share, the change needed to reach your target and how much the whole portfolio would move if crypto prices fell or rose.

```calculator
crypto-allocation
```

## How the Crypto Allocation Calculator Works

Crypto share is crypto value divided by total portfolio value. The change to reach a target is the target share times the total, minus current crypto holdings. In the price scenarios, only the crypto portion changes. With the defaults, $10,000 of crypto in a $100,000 portfolio is a 10% share; reaching 5% would mean reducing crypto by $5,000; a 50% crypto price fall would lower the whole portfolio by about 5%. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why Exposure Counts More Than Price

The impact of a crypto price swing on your overall finances depends on how much of your portfolio it represents. A large fall in a small position has a limited effect on the total, while the same fall in a large position can change your plans. ## Choosing a Target

The target share is your own decision. This tool shows exposure and does not recommend buying, selling or holding any asset or a specific allocation. Your time horizon, other savings and ability to absorb losses all affect what level suits you.

## What the Calculator Leaves Out

Correlation between crypto and other investments, fees, taxes and changes in non-crypto holdings are not modeled.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How Do I Calculate My Crypto Allocation?

Divide the value of your crypto holdings by the total value of all your investments, then multiply by 100 for a percentage.

### How Would a Crypto Crash Affect My Portfolio?

Multiply the crypto price fall by your crypto share. A 50% fall in crypto that makes up 10% of your portfolio lowers the total by about 5%.

### What Percentage of a Portfolio Should Be in Crypto?

This tool does not suggest one. The right level depends on your goals, time horizon and ability to absorb losses, so it is a personal decision.
