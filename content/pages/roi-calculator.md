# ROI Calculator: Return on Investment and Annualized ROI

This ROI calculator measures the return on an investment, project or purchase. Enter what you put in, any other costs and the final value to see the net gain, the total ROI and the annualized return over the holding period.

```calculator
roi
```

## How ROI Is Calculated

ROI equals the net gain divided by the total cost. Investing $10,000 with $500 of other costs and ending with $14,000 gives a $3,500 gain on $10,500, an ROI of 33.33%. These are Fin Loom calculations from the inputs shown.

## Why Annualized ROI Counts

A total ROI does not show how long the return took. Annualized ROI spreads it over the holding period. The 33.33% return over 3 years works out to about 10.06% a year, which makes it easier to compare with other options.

## Limits of a Simple ROI

The calculation assumes all money went in at the start and came out at the end. Investments with cash flows along the way, such as rental income or added contributions, need a method that weighs each cash flow by its timing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What Is a Good ROI?

It depends on the risk, time frame and alternatives. Compare annualized ROI with what similar-risk options could earn over the same period.

### What Is the Difference Between ROI and Annualized ROI?

ROI is the total return over the whole period. Annualized ROI converts it to a yearly rate, so returns over different lengths of time can be compared.

### Should I Include Fees in ROI?

Yes. Fees and other costs are part of what you put in. Leaving them out makes the return look higher than it was.
